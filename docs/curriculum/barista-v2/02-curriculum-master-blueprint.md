# CherryEdu Barista Specialization V2 — Curriculum & Learning Experience Architecture

**Stage:** 02 — sequence audited and re-ordered (12 modules / 68 lesson slots), signature R&D and experimentation as core; **lessons are still being authored, not deployed or human-validated**  
**Mission:** Develop **independent barista judgment under changing conditions** (not obedience to one recipe), safe and repeatable coffee service, **and commercially viable, original signature-beverage development**: define a creative brief, prototype coffee-led drinks, validate with real customers, cost, document and hand off an original recipe that another barista can reproduce.  
**Audience:** Foundation-level learners transitioning to bar work, working baristas needing a repeatable SOP, coffee shop teams; also supports no-machine learners with honest limits.  
**Do not claim:** That an online-only completion proves professional bar competence or awards SCA/BNSP credentials.

## 1. Pedagogical contract (must pass on EACH lesson)

- **First mental model, then terminology.** Show the physical object and purpose (portafilter, filter basket, group, burr) BEFORE grind distribution formulas and pressure curves.
- **Principle before prescription.** The learner must explain what each variable controls, what evidence to observe, and what tradeoff a change may introduce. Recipes supplied by a head barista are useful operational baselines, **not universal laws**.
- **Customer-responsive autonomy.** Teach clarification of ambiguous requests (stronger, less acidic, lighter, no dairy), safe substitutions and permitted recipe adjustments. The student must be able to ask the right follow-up question, not guess their meaning.
- **Curiosity and experimentation are trained habits.** Invite learner-owned 'what if?' questions across the course; learners hypothesize, predict, control variables, observe, explain surprises and propose next trials. An experiment that disproves a hypothesis can still be excellent learning.
- **Ask → hypothesize → predict → change → observe → interpret → repeat.** Each lesson builds a mental model and a genuine decision; a quiz or two-column 'card' cannot substitute for experimentation. Actual tasting evidence requires tasting; hypothetical simulation must be labeled.
- **No fake cards.** Distinct interaction types need real state and feedback, meaningful affordances, progressive reveal and keyboard/phone support. A long bulleted explanation is never presented as an interactive activity.
- **Creative agency is core.** Learners must propose, prototype, evaluate, name, cost, operationalize and explain their own signature drink—not just memorize popular syrup pairings or replicate an instructor's menu.
- **Theoretical knowledge and real skill are different claims.** Case-based desk exercises are genuine learning, but cannot establish real steaming, espresso, sanitation or customer service competence.
- **No one magical number.** 1:15 for filter coffee, 1:2 for espresso, 9 bar, 25–30 seconds, or any example temperature are **working reference points, not universal best outcomes**. State the intended beverage, roast/bean, brew method, grinder, water, sensory outcome, throughput and guest request. Explain that changing the water-to-coffee ratio can change concentration *and* extraction when brew water passes through the bed; bypass dilution is a different operation. Adjust in controlled trials, measure and taste again. Equipment and food-safety boundaries remain non-negotiable.
- **Avoid false certainty in diagnosis.** One sour shot does not prove under-extraction, and one slow shot does not prove too-fine grind. Require multiple observations, competing hypotheses and meaningful checks, including machine, water, puck and customer preference.
- **Safe SOP flexibility.** Respect menu specification, allergen/hygiene controls and authorized cafe procedures. Propose a tested adjustment within the venue's scope or escalate; do not teach unauthorized improvisation as competence.
- **Safety-first gating.** Hot steam, pressure, chemicals, allergens, electrical faults, spoiled ingredients: stop-work and trained-personnel escalation have precedence over speed and beautiful latte art.
- **Evidence & source QA.** Each lesson has learner-facing educational content PLUS separate references/claim-review appendix with publication or edition. Technical advice is reviewed by qualified barista trainer and relevant hygiene/safety specialist.
- **Visuals that teach.** Espressogram/flow graph, real portafilter cutaway, grind distribution side-by-side, milk microfoam close-up, steam wand positions, pour sequence and physical bar work triangle; no stock images pretending to demonstrate equipment technique.
- **No answer leakage.** Model solutions unlock after authentic attempt; scenario data differs from worked examples; final exams have plausible distractors without length-key cues.
- **Plain, humane copy.** “Barista Specialization: Keterampilan Bar & Espresso” is a working CUSTOMER-FACING title candidate. Do not use “Sertifikasi Profesional Kompetensi Bar” before independent credential authorization.

## Design principle: teach barista reasoning, not recipe obedience

**North-star ability (Bahasa Indonesia):** "Barista bisa menjelaskan mengapa sebuah resep cocok pada kondisi tertentu, mengenali kapan kondisi berubah, dan mengambil keputusan berbasis bukti tanpa melanggar keamanan atau prosedur kerja." The complete reusable diagnostic method, scenario bank and acceptance rubric are maintained in [Adaptive Barista Reasoning Standard](./04-adaptive-barista-reasoning-standard.md).

Every lesson and final assessment must distinguish:
- **What must stay safe**: food hygiene, allergens, machine/electrical/steam hazards, cafe authorizations.
- **What can be adjusted**: grinding, dose, yield, ratio, extraction process, water, temperature (within approved equipment/food conditions), sensory balance, drink assembly and service flow.
- **What cannot be assumed**: a unique 'perfect' recipe, cause inferred from a single symptom, a guaranteed sensory effect, or guest meaning behind an ambiguous description.

**Six-step learner method:** Clarify desired outcome → observe present constraints → rule out safety/authority violations → form plausible hypotheses → change one meaningful variable and measure/taste → explain tradeoffs, record and iterate. Under ambiguous results, choose another diagnostic test or escalate instead of performing arbitrary changes.

**Non-negotiable QA:** A beautiful step-by-step recipe, an attractive image and even a working dose/yield calculator are insufficient if the learner cannot **transfer** the principle to a new scenario.

## Curiosity through experimentation — cross-course design contract

Curiosity means **active question formation and willingness to revise beliefs based on evidence**, not random mixing, excessive spending, speed or personality. Students learn to ask their own 'what if?' questions even in everyday bar work.

A complete experiment includes: (1) question, (2) hypothesis plus prediction, (3) controlled variable(s) and context, (4) safe method, (5) actual measurement or clearly marked simulated data, (6) interpretation including uncertainty, and (7) a useful next trial. *A failed hypothesis is not a failed learner.*

**Coverage:** meaningful hypothesis/prediction opportunities across skill lessons; at least one documented appropriately scoped experiment per module. Build an ongoing notebook and use it for the signature-drink R&D portfolio and unseen capstone cases. No fixed number of shots, costly ingredients or equipment ownership required. Never invent sensory results or encourage unauthorized experiments during service.

**Observe curiosity as behavior**: asks meaningful questions, designs careful tests, considers surprising outcomes, changes their mind with evidence, records constraints and knows when to stop/escalate safely. Do not give learners personality scores or automatically reward novelty over safety.

Implementation and QA details: [Experimentation & Curiosity Standard](./05-experimentation-and-curiosity-standard.md).

## 2. Proposed learning stages

| Stage | Meaning | Valid evidence |
|---|---|---|
| S1 — Understand | Can identify components, explain principles, recognize hazards | scenario classification + explanation in own words |
| S2 — Practice (guided) | Follows a reproducible protocol in a scenario or with optional real gear | simulator trace / guided observations / log |
| S3 — Diagnose | Can interpret inconsistent output and select a controlled next action | unseen-case reasoning, not trivia |
| S4 — Demonstrate | Can physically prepare, taste, texture milk, clean and serve under observation | trained human review / timestamped practical evidence with identity + site safety protocols |
| S5 — Transfer | Can apply skill across shift, coffee, equipment and customer variations | holistic service capstone and repeat trials |

S1–S3 can be taught online. S4–S5 cannot be signed off by reading, a chatbot, a photo or MCQ alone.

## 3. Accepted dependency-first sequence — 12 modules / 68 lesson slots

**Decision (sequence audit, 13 October 2026):** The old 11-module sequence was **not pedagogically optimal**. Hygiene/service should begin before the first machine practice; controlled experimentation should precede advanced machine diagnostics; early service needs recur later in higher-complexity customer cases; equipment care and costing each deserve their own assessed stage. A distinction is made between early safe cleaning (M01 and always) and later preventive machine maintenance (M09). See [sequence-dependency audit](./06-sequence-dependency-audit.md).

The following are **V2 editorial IDs**, not the production `mod-b1`... identifiers. No existing payment, learner record, certification or progress is migrated by this plan.

### BV2.M01 — Area Bar, Keselamatan & Sikap Melayani (6 lessons)
1. Peran barista, tujuan pelanggan dan perjalanan satu pesanan dari masuk hingga tersaji.
2. Peta bar: peralatan, alur bahan, zona aman/basah/panas dan fungsi tiap stasiun.
3. Kebersihan, bahan layak pakai, tangan/peralatan, alergi dan batas tanggung jawab.
4. Membuka bar: cek awal alat dan stok sebelum memulai pembuatan minuman.
5. Mendengarkan pelanggan: pertanyaan klarifikasi saat permintaan 'strong', 'tidak asam' atau 'tanpa susu' ambigu.
6. Simulasi satu order pertama, handoff, penutupan ringan, dan satu hipotesis peningkatan alur.

**Early gate:** dapat mengenali situasi stop-work dan alergi sebelum interaksi alat; membedakan pesanan pelanggan dari asumsi barista. **Media:** foto nyata stasiun + peta interaktif, demonstrasi cuci alat, order flow. **Aktivitas:** klasifikasi zona/alat, kasus alergi, sequence ticket dan eksperimen tata letak aman.

### BV2.M02 — Fundamental Ekstraksi & Cara Bereksperimen (5 lessons)
1. Apa yang berpindah dari bubuk ke air: ekstraksi, konsentrasi, rasa dan keterbatasan sensori.
2. Memahami rasio, satuan, brew water vs beverage, dan alasan 1:15 bukan hukum.
3. Mengapa grind, suhu, waktu, aliran dan kualitas air mengubah proses; satu variabel bukan jawaban tunggal.
4. Percobaan pertama: pertanyaan sendiri → hipotesis → perbandingan terkendali → bukti.
5. Membaca hasil tak terduga dan menerapkan prinsip pada kopi/alat/tujuan pelanggan yang berubah.

**Gate:** mampu mendesain eksperimen sederhana dan menjelaskan mekanisme serta ketidakpastian; tidak perlu memiliki mesin espresso. **Media:** ilustrasi perpindahan zat terlarut dan perbandingan skenario dengan log terukur. **Aktivitas:** dose/water ratio lab, prediction notebook, counterexample transfer.

### BV2.M03 — Grinder, Portafilter & Espresso Pertama (6 lessons)
1. Grinder, burr, retention, setting dan keselamatan praktis.
2. Dose, yield, beverage ratio, shot time; mengenali satuan dan konsekuensinya.
3. Basket, distribusi, tamping, aliran dan keterbatasan interpretasi puck.
4. Memahami kontrol mesin dan petunjuk produsen; menghindari resep '9 bar wajib'.
5. Menarik shot awal dan merekam data serta pengamatan dengan aman.
6. Membandingkan dua resep dan memprediksi dampak sebelum mencoba penyesuaian.

**Gate:** membaca shot log dan membuat espresso sesuai batas SOP latihan. **Media:** bagian grinder/portafilter nyata, video proses, diagram aliran. **Aktivitas:** urutkan langkah, unit-safe shot log, prediksi dampak variabel.

### BV2.M04 — Dial-In, Sensorik Espresso & Troubleshooting (7 lessons)
1. Bedakan konsentrasi dan extraction yield dengan persepsi rasa.
2. Sensorik espresso: aroma, acidity, sweetness, bitterness dan body tanpa diagnosis otomatis.
3. Membangun hipotesis dari shot cepat/lambat: grind, dose, puck dan kondisi mesin.
4. Mengubah satu parameter dalam uji A/B, mencatat tradeoff dan sumber ketidakpastian.
5. Espresso berubah antarlot dan antargiliran; memilih pengujian paling informatif.
6. Quality-control decision: keep, adjust, remake, hold atau escalate.
7. Blind/controlled comparative lab dan ujian kasus baru dengan beberapa solusi defensibel.

**Gate:** menyelesaikan diagnosis shot yang belum pernah dilihat dan mengusulkan eksperimen aman yang masuk akal. **Media:** shot log, video aliran, anotasi puck; simulasi tidak boleh mengarang prediksi sensorik pasti.

### BV2.M05 — Susu, Steaming, Tekstur & Alergen (6 lessons)
1. Komposisi susu sapi dan alternatif nabati, alergi, preferensi dan pelabelan.
2. Steam wand dan pitcher: batas aman, teknik dan prosedur higienis.
3. Aeration vs texturing, posisi wand dan efek pada ukuran gelembung.
4. Eksperimen aman perbandingan dua kondisi texturing dan interpretasi hasil.
5. Mendiagnosis tekstur, suhu dan ketidaksesuaian minuman, tanpa kesimpulan tunggal.
6. Menyelesaikan satu minuman susu dan membersihkan stasiun secara aman.

**Gate:** keamanan alergi dan sanitasi adalah kegagalan kritis; pemahaman teori ≠ bukti keterampilan fisik. **Media:** footage nyata wand-pitcher dan microfoam.

### BV2.M06 — Penyusunan Minuman & Dasar Latte Art (5 lessons)
1. Membaca resep café, variasi menu espresso dan komunikasi ukuran/rasa.
2. Merakit espresso, air, susu dan es sesuai tujuan pelanggan.
3. Menuang secara bertahap; dasar heart dan tulip, fokus tekstur/hasil minuman lebih dulu.
4. Menganalisis kesalahan: suhu, tekstur, dilution, penyajian vs estetika.
5. Mini-ideation: merancang variasi minuman dengan alasan sensori dan uji terukur.

**Gate:** menu/hasil konsisten, tidak memaksakan swan atau latte art sebagai syarat kualitas. **Media:** real pour series dan drink construction map.

### BV2.M07 — Filter, Batch Brewing & Ritme Layanan (5 lessons)
1. Mempraktikkan kembali ekstraksi M02 dalam V60, immersion atau brewer setara.
2. Menyesuaikan grind, rasio, pola aliran, air dan waktu berdasarkan pertanyaan pelanggan.
3. Memilih manual, immersion, batch atau bypass berdasarkan target dan kendala.
4. Menyajikan banyak pesanan, menjaga identitas tiket, alat dan repetabilitas.
5. Laboratorium dua alat atau dua kondisi, lalu menganalisis hasil tak terduga.

**Gate:** tidak sekadar memakai 1:15; mampu menjelaskan penyesuaian dan batas inferensi. **Media:** flow map dan simulasi antrean.

### BV2.M08 — Hospitality Adaptif & Permintaan Tidak Biasa (5 lessons)
1. Menjelaskan karakter kopi tanpa jargon, menggali preferensi dan ketidakpastian.
2. Mengklarifikasi 'lebih strong', 'less acidic', tanpa susu atau alergi sebelum memberi pilihan.
3. Mengatasi komplain dan remake tanpa menyalahkan tamu, dalam kewenangan kedai.
4. Mengelola rush hour, handoff shift dan konflik prioritas keselamatan.
5. Ujian role-play pelanggan unik: dua alternatif, tradeoff, komunikasi dan pembenaran.

**Gate:** mampu bertanya, membedakan alergi dari preferensi, menjelaskan pilihan dan eskalasi. **Media:** skenario dialog dengan konsekuensi yang sebenarnya.

### BV2.M09 — Cleaning, Preventive Care & Fault Escalation (5 lessons)
1. Dari kebersihan harian M01 menuju jadwal sanitasi per shift/harian/berkala.
2. Pembersihan grinder, group, steam wand dan alat menurut manual model yang benar.
3. Label bahan kimia, penyimpanan, APD, dan hal yang tidak boleh dilakukan barista.
4. Filter air, kerak, gasket/leak, anomali tekanan: pengamatan vs pekerjaan teknisi.
5. Simulasi insiden: stop work, pelaporan dan restart sesuai izin.

**Gate:** keselamatan tidak ditunda sampai modul ini; **barista tidak mengutak-atik boiler/kelistrikan internal**. **Media:** demonstration cleaning yang diverifikasi produsen.

### BV2.M10 — Bahan, Inventory, Waste & Cost (5 lessons)
1. Membaca pemasok, tanggal, lot, label dan stock rotation FEFO/FIFO.
2. Cold chain dan penanganan bahan sensitif serta alergen.
3. Mengukur dose waste, milk waste, yield dan menyusun log.
4. Menghitung HPP per gelas, harga dan margin dengan satuan serta asumsi.
5. Eksperimen efisiensi: substitusi, stockout, biaya dan konsekuensi rasa/service.

**Gate:** unit dan asumsi benar; tidak memakai angka ekonomi palsu untuk membenarkan minuman.

### BV2.M11 — Signature Drink R&D Studio (8 lessons) — CORE
1. Temukan kebutuhan pelanggan dan celah menu; susun creative brief terukur.
2. Pelajari peran espresso/filter, rasa, tekstur, suhu dan ingredient pairing; kembangkan tiga ide berbeda.
3. Verifikasi bahan, seasonality, alergen, kesesuaian teknik dan keamanan penyimpanan.
4. Buat hipotesis, V0–V3 terukur, sesi tasting atau simulasi jujur, dan analisis hasil yang berbeda dari prediksi.
5. Hitung HPP, usable yield, waste, packaging, harga dan sensitivitas biaya.
6. Tulis resep/SOP yang dapat direplikasi orang lain dengan pengendalian mutu dan batas hold.
7. Nama, menu description, garnish/visual, service script dan komunikasi rasa yang faktual.
8. Customer trial, second-barista reproduction, lalu keputusan keep / revise / rotate / retire dengan bukti.

**Gate:** original coffee-led signature drink dengan eksperimen, keselamatan, alasan rasa, biaya dan uji replikasi. **Desk-only learners:** concept portfolio, bukan klaim telah mencicipi atau meluncurkan produk.

### BV2.M12 — Bar Service, Adaptation & Innovation Capstone (5 lessons)
1. Venue brief: satu shift baru, alat, pelanggan, menu, aturan keselamatan dan data stok.
2. Opening sampai order masuk: mengenali konteks, mengajukan pertanyaan, melayani.
3. Perubahan mendadak: kopi/grinder, permintaan unik, alergen dan stockout sekaligus.
4. Mencari penyebab, memilih uji berikutnya, memperbaiki resep atau menahan penyajian, mencatat HPP dan waste.
5. Portofolio keputusan mandiri, signature drink, eksperimen, handoff dan rencana pembelajaran lanjutan.

**Gate:** mampu berpikir tanpa instruksi resep tunggal; bukti keterampilan praktik diverifikasi terpisah dari tes online.

### Optional future electives (not gates to basic job readiness)

Advanced swan/competition latte art; specialist molecular techniques (clarification, controlled fermentation, carbonation) **only with demonstrated food-safety expertise**; café leadership/roster and hiring. **Fundamental signature drink creation is NOT an elective.** These need separate skill prerequisites, safe facilities and source review.

## 4. Types of meaningful interaction (purpose, not decoration)

| Widget / interaction | Needed for | Attempt-first mechanism / QA |
|---|---|---|
| **Equipment explorer** | Identify parts and safe handling | Tap real photographed point; ask action/function BEFORE revealing label |
| **Grinder / extraction log simulator** | Dose, yield, time; adjust variables | Learner edits 1 parameter, sees credible synthetic response *marked as model*; no claim of prediction-grade precision |
| **Puck prep diagnosis** | Distribution, clumps, tamp | Choose probable problems from photos; distinguish observation from unproven causation |
| **Steam wand stage explorer** | Aeration / texture / hygiene | Annotated **real video** with timed decision points; sound/captions and no blind “hot enough” reliance |
| **Bar ticket queue** | Teamwork, sequencing | User prioritizes tickets, handles allergen flagged order, simulated timed feedback but no unsafe speed-first scoring |
| **Cold chain & allergen check** | Critical sanitation | Explicit critical fail vs explanatory knowledge checks; no false operational green clearance |
| **Opening–closing checklist** | Safe routine | Item states + why; practice in browser, optional real supervised signature |
| **QC decision workbench** | Calibration and honest uncertainty | Evidence log, missing data choices, independent explanation then model comparison |
| **Curiosity Lab / Experiment Notebook** | Build independent experimental thinking | Learner-owned question, falsifiable hypothesis, prediction, controlled variables, measurement, observation, uncertainty, interpretation and next trial; accept defensible negative results |
| **Waste and cost calculator** | Operational math | Units required; finite and realistic amounts, clear assumptions, no silent overflow |
| **Signature flavor canvas** | Build an original concept from sensory evidence | Choose intended customer experience, coffee anchor, flavor structure, ingredients and rationale before any model answer; no unsupported automatic 'taste score' |
| **Recipe experiment log** | Compare meaningful prototypes | Editable gram/ml units, variable tracking, anonymized tasting observations, repeat-trial history, explicit 'hypothetical' mark for simulated data |
| **Signature service stress-test** | Operational handoff of a unique drink | Simulate queue, missing ingredient, cost change, cleaning, allergen warning; learner decides revise/hold/launch with reasoning |
| **Role-play dialogues** | Hospitality | Nuanced response branches, language options and no stereotype categories |

If a visual would **materially teach physical mechanics**, a text-only fallback may help access but never become the sole instructional medium. Do not fabricate realistic videos and present them as documentary evidence.

## 5. Assessments & competency claims

### Theory assessment
- Each module needs a diversified item blueprint: procedure ordering, visual interpretation, counterexample, calculations, incident triage, multi-variable scenario, and explanations.
- **Signature Lab mastery is portfolio/project-based**, not multiple-choice alone. Learner must invent at least one original coffee-based beverage with an explicit brief, prototype log, assessed tasting, precise recipe, truthful product story, allergen/safety controls, costing and another person's reproduction; online scenario-only version must be named non-practical.
- No mandatory fixed 3 MCQ per module. Start with an item pool sized to objectives and cognitive load, then **calibrate empirically**; never force quantity over validity.
- Make 4-option MCQs have plausible wrong answers with similar length and voice; run longest-key, first-word, answer-position, giveaway and repeated-pattern checks.
- Store correct answers and rationale in protected question bank, not leaked in initial presentation; don't claim exam integrity if all answers are client bundled.
- Module exit must assess **transfer on an unseen case**, not memorized working examples. Each tested concept must include at least one altered condition and a defensible decision with explanatory rationale, uncertainty and follow-up measurement. Do not automatically mark a defensible alternative wrong because it differs from the head barista's recipe.
- No single-score quiz can substitute for a **customer request clarification + safe tradeoff + hypothesis + proposed test**. Unusual customer requests are essential competency cases across the course, not a single hospitality-only topic.
- Retries require item variants and feedback timing rules; preserve existing learners' historic attempts/version.

### Practical verification
- Separate hands-on rubric: safety and hygiene (critical), recipe fidelity, taste and repeatability, milk texture and allergen precautions, time/workflow, service, cleaning and escalation.
- **Signature evidence:** 3 purposeful variants (or justified smaller designs when ingredient constraints apply), two independent making/tasting sessions, blind or preference-balanced feedback where practical, an approved recipe card, and a second person reproducing it; assess creativity **and operational viability** separately.
- Specify exact safe equipment context, permitted reviewer role, consent, identity verification, required repeated attempts and artifact privacy before accepting any video or on-site evidence.
- If gear is unavailable, the learner can finish the **online theory path**, but cannot be marked practically competent.
- The platform may later issue a **clearly scoped CherryEdu course completion record**, not accredited professional competency certificate, after reliable centralized completion persistence is implemented.

### Credential notice (public copy)
"Belajar keterampilan barista melalui materi, simulasi, dan latihan. Sertifikat kompetensi profesi resmi memerlukan asesmen praktik melalui penyelenggara yang berwenang."  
Show this short, honest distinction in relevant course detail and graduation FAQ, not internal status jargon or repetitive badges on every lesson.

## Continuous menu innovation — keep Barista skills current

Barista students must be able to repeat the R&D cycle **after the course**. Fast-moving drink trends are **case studies, not permanent recipes to memorize**. Keep two distinct content layers: (1) evergreen flavor, extraction, hospitality, costing, food-safety and experiment principles; and (2) a dated, editable example library of seasonal drinks, menu formats, local ingredients and customer preferences.

- **Every 3 months (editorial review):** verify current menus and industry publications, local guest/customer evidence, ingredient availability and safety requirements. Add or retire examples with dated sources, documented reviewer and clearly limited geographic relevance. Do not turn a US consumer survey into an assertion about Indonesian customers.
- **Optional monthly learner challenge:** brief → 2 concepts → prototype → feedback → cost/service test → pitch → keep/revise/retire. Learners build a versioned signature portfolio, not just win quizzes.
- **Rotating "Barista's Choice" service scenario:** customer asks about taste, caffeine and allergens; barista uses a controlled, documented recipe family. Improvisation cannot waive allergen controls or safe prep.
- **Production-feedback loop for café teams:** actual trial requires customer consent, anonymized feedback, prep time, waste, repeatability and permission to publish staff recipe attribution. Without a real trial, show an explicitly fictional teaching dataset and no invented sales results.
- **Curriculum governance:** new trend examples pass scientific, food-safety, learner-copy, media-rights and mobile UI QA before publication. Stable theory modules do not get rewritten simply because a flavor is trending.

**Future implementation notes:** Learner-authored recipe versioning, ingredients with units and allergens, controlled trial logs, costing, peer handoff and evidence source dates should be extensible independent records—not hardcoded 2026 examples. A proposed or simulated recipe must never be labeled production-validated, independently tasted or officially certified.

**Evidence:** SCA Barista Skills Professional custom drink outcome (https://education.sca.coffee/coffee-skills-program); WCC signature beverage category (https://wcc.coffee/rules-regulations); National Restaurant Association 2026 Beverage Trends (US-specific context, https://www.restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/research-reports/restaurant-beverage-trends-report/); Indonesian secondary menu examples above.
