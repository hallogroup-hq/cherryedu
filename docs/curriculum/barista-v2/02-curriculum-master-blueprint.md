# CherryEdu Barista Specialization V2 — Curriculum & Learning Experience Architecture

**Stage:** 02 — blueprint candidate, not implemented or student-reviewed  
**Mission:** From first safe shift behind the bar to a repeatable, observable ability to prepare and serve coffee, diagnose defects, manage work and communicate confidently.  
**Audience:** Foundation-level learners transitioning to bar work, working baristas needing a repeatable SOP, coffee shop teams; also supports no-machine learners with honest limits.  
**Do not claim:** That an online-only completion proves professional bar competence or awards SCA/BNSP credentials.

## 1. Pedagogical contract (must pass on EACH lesson)

- **First mental model, then terminology.** Show the physical object and purpose (portafilter, filter basket, group, burr) BEFORE grind distribution formulas and pressure curves.
- **Observe → predict → act → measure → decide → reflect.** Each lesson ends in an observable task, not an arbitrary 2-column “quiz”.
- **No fake cards.** Distinct interaction types need real state and feedback, meaningful affordances, progressive reveal and keyboard/phone support. A long bulleted explanation is never presented as an interactive activity.
- **Theoretical knowledge and real skill are different claims.** Case-based desk exercises are genuine learning, but cannot establish real steaming, espresso, sanitation or customer service competence.
- **No one magical number.** 9 bar, 1:2, 25–30 seconds, 55–65°C etc are contextual/illustrative, NOT automatically universal standards. Label recipe as example and teach controlled experimentation; safety ranges follow ingredient/equipment context.
- **Safety-first gating.** Hot steam, pressure, chemicals, allergens, electrical faults, spoiled ingredients: stop-work and trained-personnel escalation have precedence over speed and beautiful latte art.
- **Evidence & source QA.** Each lesson has learner-facing educational content PLUS separate references/claim-review appendix with publication or edition. Technical advice is reviewed by qualified barista trainer and relevant hygiene/safety specialist.
- **Visuals that teach.** Espressogram/flow graph, real portafilter cutaway, grind distribution side-by-side, milk microfoam close-up, steam wand positions, pour sequence and physical bar work triangle; no stock images pretending to demonstrate equipment technique.
- **No answer leakage.** Model solutions unlock after authentic attempt; scenario data differs from worked examples; final exams have plausible distractors without length-key cues.
- **Plain, humane copy.** “Barista Specialization: Keterampilan Bar & Espresso” is a working CUSTOMER-FACING title candidate. Do not use “Sertifikasi Profesional Kompetensi Bar” before independent credential authorization.

## 2. Proposed learning stages

| Stage | Meaning | Valid evidence |
|---|---|---|
| S1 — Understand | Can identify components, explain principles, recognize hazards | scenario classification + explanation in own words |
| S2 — Practice (guided) | Follows a reproducible protocol in a scenario or with optional real gear | simulator trace / guided observations / log |
| S3 — Diagnose | Can interpret inconsistent output and select a controlled next action | unseen-case reasoning, not trivia |
| S4 — Demonstrate | Can physically prepare, taste, texture milk, clean and serve under observation | trained human review / timestamped practical evidence with identity + site safety protocols |
| S5 — Transfer | Can apply skill across shift, coffee, equipment and customer variations | holistic service capstone and repeat trials |

S1–S3 can be taught online. S4–S5 cannot be signed off by reading, a chatbot, a photo or MCQ alone.

## 3. Proposed 10-module sequencing (~55 lesson slots, count not a proxy for depth)

The original production identifiers `mod-b1`–`mod-b10` are NOT to be silently repurposed. Codes below are **editorial proposal IDs only** (`BV2.Mxx`), to be versioned separately until migration is approved.

### BV2.M01 — Memulai Kerja di Bar (5 lessons)
1. Meet the workstation: cup, portafilter, basket, grinder, group, knock box, steam wand, scale — identify function using real labeled visuals.
2. Opening with safety: dry hands, water/steam/electrical zones, PPE where required, safe emergency escalation.
3. Food safety, safe ingredients and allergen communication; separate clean/dirty areas.
4. Mise en place and movement: practical start-of-shift order using interactive sequence.
5. First complete order walk-through, slower and repeatable before timing for speed.

**Mastery:** Set up a fictional workstation in logical safe order; identify stop-work hazards; sequence a complete order without skipping hygiene. **Media:** actual photographed bar station + interactive map. **Practical:** supervised workstation setup.

### BV2.M02 — Menguasai Grinder & Espresso Pertama (7 lessons)
1. Bean freshness/recipe as practical inputs, no premature origin deep dive.
2. Burr grinder, adjustment mechanisms, retention/purging and safe handling.
3. Dose (g), beverage mass/yield (g), time (s), brew ratio — distinguish all units.
4. Distribution and tamping: puck preparation sequences and effect of variation.
5. Basic machine controls, group and portafilter; examples not “always 9 bar”.
6. Pull and record first shots; a minimally sufficient observation log.
7. Intro dial-in: comparison of two shots, hold everything except one change.

**Mastery:** Learner chooses a next intervention using a given shot log, records plausible settings and knows what remains unmeasured. **Media:** puck cross-section, grinder settings and extraction clip. **Practical:** supervised safe grinder/espresso operation.

### BV2.M03 — Dial-In, Troubleshooting, & Espresso QC (6 lessons)
1. Distinguish concentration, extraction, beverage mass, time and flavor.
2. Assess acidity, bitterness, balance and body without “sour = under” certainty.
3. Channeling and puck defects: what a bottomless shot can/cannot prove.
4. Adjust recipe one variable at a time; handle changing beans/environment.
5. Diagnose multi-factor inconsistency across 3 simulated shifts.
6. Calibration log and service decision: remake, hold, maintenance escalation.

**Mastery:** Analyze three unfamiliar espresso records and propose evidence-based, safe decisions. **Media:** side-by-side visually **real** espresso flow plus plotted logs. **Practical:** produce repeatable shots to trainer-approved recipe/tolerance, with measured variation.

### BV2.M04 — Susu, Tekstur, & Alergen (6 lessons)
1. Milk and plant-based alternatives: protein/fat differences and label reading.
2. Safe steam-wand setup; never overfill, never position hand in steam path.
3. Aeration vs texturing; position, vortex and sound — video with slow-motion controls.
4. Microfoam diagnosis: big bubbles, separation, overheated/undertextured milk.
5. Temperature, sanitation, reuse decisions and cold-chain hygiene according to policies.
6. One espresso + one textured milk drink, consistent finish and cleaning.

**Mastery:** Visually diagnose foam stages; spot allergens and contamination routes. **Media:** filmed pitcher, steam-wand detail and microfoam closeups. **Practical:** trainer-observed steam/texture and cleanup with actual thermometry and safe practices.

### BV2.M05 — Menu Espresso & Latte Art (5 lessons)
1. Beverage names as service recipes: espresso, Americano, milk drinks; local variations are acknowledged.
2. Mixing, glassware, order details, temperature and serve-time decisions.
3. Pouring foundation: high vs low pour and pitcher angle (video).
4. Heart to tulip progression with iterative checkpoints, not rote “swan or fail”.
5. Quality criteria beyond aesthetics: drink spec, taste, cleanliness, guest preference.

**Mastery:** Assign order to recipe and distinguish taste/temperature/safety errors from surface pattern aesthetics. **Media:** real pour series. **Practical:** latte art judged as a progressive skill, not essential substitute for safe correct beverage.

### BV2.M06 — Filter & Batch Brewing untuk Layanan Bar (5 lessons)
1. Filter setup & a reproducible recipe; equipment and water context.
2. Grind, ratio, pour, brew time and sensory feedback, distinguishing controls from observations.
3. Choose immersion/percolation/batch service by constraints.
4. Serving multiple manual brews without losing measurements/labels.
5. Diagnose batch-brew concentration and bypass/holding limitations safely.

**Mastery:** Adapt a service plan to a plausible ticket queue, maintaining order accuracy. **Media:** flow path comparison and queue simulator. **Practical:** measured filter brew repeatability.

### BV2.M07 — Equipment Care & Preventive Maintenance (6 lessons)
1. User vs technician boundary: what the barista is **not** authorized to open.
2. Safe shutdown and machine manufacturer manual lookup.
3. Daily/shift/weekly group and portafilter cleaning—chemical labels, correct dilution and PPE.
4. Steam wand cleaning, food-contact surfaces and cross-contamination prevention.
5. Water filters, scale risk, backflush applicability **only if manufacturer allows**, equipment log.
6. Fault escalation simulation: leaks, electrical odor, pressure anomalies, damaged seals.

**Mastery:** Correctly sort barista-safe actions vs service technician escalation. **Media:** verified equipment model, hazard icon/photo and maintenance checklist. **Practical:** trainer-observed cleaning and lockout procedure.

### BV2.M08 — Hospitality & High-Volume Service (5 lessons)
1. Ask good questions and explain coffee flavors without intimidating jargon.
2. Allergen, intolerance and customer preference communication using neutral language.
3. Handle a customer complaint without blame; clear remake policy.
4. Ticket/order sequencing, teammate handoff and cross-shift traceability.
5. Peak-hour simulation: throughput without skipping temperature, cleaning or identity labels.

**Mastery:** Role-play new customer cases and prioritize safety over speed. **Media:** interactive POS order queue and a real service video. **Practical:** observed service role-play.

### BV2.M09 — Inventory, Yield, Waste & Cost (5 lessons)
1. FIFO vs FEFO; why expiration, batch and storage condition matter.
2. Milk handling, refrigerated logs, labeling and supplier checks.
3. Weigh and calculate grinder retention, dial-in wastage and service yield.
4. Cost per drink vs selling price: cost inputs and uncertainty.
5. Opening/closing audit, cash/POS control boundaries and incident log.

**Mastery:** Process fictional invoices, stock rotations and ingredient-loss records; avoid handling real user financial data. **Media:** inventory worksheet and waste calculator. **Practical:** daily stock station check.

### BV2.M10 — Bar Service Capstone & Career Progression (5 lessons)
1. Case intake: venue layout, staff roles, menu and equipment constraints.
2. Service scenario A: opening and normal shift with two order types.
3. Service scenario B: espresso drifts, milk allergen uncertainty, concurrent customer complaint.
4. Diagnose/recover: quality log, stop-work calls, technician escalation, cost record.
5. Complete reflective dossier, performance rubric and individualized next-step plan.

**Mastery:** Demonstrate coherent, defensible bar decisions under changing conditions. **Digital capstone:** document decisions with independent attempt and counterfactual discussion. **Official workplace competence:** **NOT established** without verified practical assessment.

### Optional future electives (not gates to basic job readiness)

Advanced swan and competition latte art; milk clarification and controlled food-safe signature beverages; café leadership/roster and hiring. These need separate skill prerequisites, safe facilities and source review.

## 4. Types of meaningful interaction (purpose, not decoration)

| Widget / interaction | Needed for | Attempt-first mechanism / QA |
|---|---|---|
| **Equipment explorer** | Identify parts and safe handling | Tap real photographed point; ask action/function BEFORE revealing label |
| **Grinder / extraction log simulator** | Dose, yield, time; adjust variables | Learner edits 1 parameter, sees credible synthetic response *marked as model*; no claim of prediction-grade precision |
| **Puck prep diagnosis** | Distribution, clumps, tamp | Choose probable problems from photos; distinguish observation from unproven causation |
| **Steam wand stage explorer** | Aeration / texture / hygiene | Annotated **real video** with timed decision points; sound/captions and no blind “hot enough” reliance |
| **Bar ticket queue** | Teamwork, sequencing | User prioritizes tickets, handles allergen flagged order, simulated timed feedback but no unsafe speed-first scoring |
| **Cold chain & allergen check** | Critical sanitation | Explicit critical fail vs explanatory knowledge checks; no false operational green clearance |
| **Opening–closing checklist** | Safe routine | Item states + why; practice in browser, optional real supervised signature |
| **QC decision workbench** | Calibration and honest uncertainty | Evidence log, missing data choices, independent explanation then model comparison |
| **Waste and cost calculator** | Operational math | Units required; finite and realistic amounts, clear assumptions, no silent overflow |
| **Role-play dialogues** | Hospitality | Nuanced response branches, language options and no stereotype categories |

If a visual would **materially teach physical mechanics**, a text-only fallback may help access but never become the sole instructional medium. Do not fabricate realistic videos and present them as documentary evidence.

## 5. Assessments & competency claims

### Theory assessment
- Each module needs a diversified item blueprint: procedure ordering, visual interpretation, counterexample, calculations, incident triage, multi-variable scenario, and explanations.
- No mandatory fixed 3 MCQ per module. Start with an item pool sized to objectives and cognitive load, then **calibrate empirically**; never force quantity over validity.
- Make 4-option MCQs have plausible wrong answers with similar length and voice; run longest-key, first-word, answer-position, giveaway and repeated-pattern checks.
- Store correct answers and rationale in protected question bank, not leaked in initial presentation; don't claim exam integrity if all answers are client bundled.
- Module exit must assess **transfer on an unseen case**, not memorized working examples.
- Retries require item variants and feedback timing rules; preserve existing learners' historic attempts/version.

### Practical verification
- Separate hands-on rubric: safety and hygiene (critical), recipe fidelity, taste and repeatability, milk texture and allergen precautions, time/workflow, service, cleaning and escalation.
- Specify exact safe equipment context, permitted reviewer role, consent, identity verification, required repeated attempts and artifact privacy before accepting any video or on-site evidence.
- If gear is unavailable, the learner can finish the **online theory path**, but cannot be marked practically competent.
- The platform may later issue a **clearly scoped CherryEdu course completion record**, not accredited professional competency certificate, after reliable centralized completion persistence is implemented.

### Credential notice (public copy)
"Belajar keterampilan barista melalui materi, simulasi, dan latihan. Sertifikat kompetensi profesi resmi memerlukan asesmen praktik melalui penyelenggara yang berwenang."  
Show this short, honest distinction in relevant course detail and graduation FAQ, not internal status jargon or repetitive badges on every lesson.
