| Lesson | Actual educational media required | Interaction that must actually work | Mastery evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M01.L01 | Order-to-cup flow showing clarification, safety and serving | Read fictional ticket, distinguish facts vs assumptions, submit response before model explanation | Explains what customer asks before choosing recipe |
| M01.L02 | Rights-cleared photograph of actual bar with accessible equipment/heat/clean-zone labels | Place tools on bar map using keyboard and pointer; detect crossovers | Explains function and risky movement |
| M01.L03 | Verified illustrated cross-contact route: milk, pitcher, wand, counter | Decide serve/check/hold with evidence; do not mark allergen-free without actual verification | Recognizes non-negotiable safety |
| M01.L04 | Opening flow with safety branch and dependency arrows | Prioritize tasks, then respond to newly revealed hazard | Can change safe task order with reasons |

## Specific transfer tasks and checks

- M01.L01: Student can ask the missing question when told a guest wants coffee 'strong'; distinguishes taste, dose and caffeine.
- M01.L02: Student can infer tool role and hazard zone from a new unfamiliar layout.
- M01.L03: Student does not offer unverified allergen-free guarantees, and can identify hold/escalation.
- M01.L04: Student reprioritizes opening safely after a new hazard is disclosed.
- M01.L05: Student proposes two honest guest options with tradeoffs; no fake sensory certainty.
- M01.L06: Student handles two tickets and spill and allergy information without dropping handoff.
- M02.L01: Student distinguishes TDS/strength from EY and liking; arithmetic percentage correct and no impossible result.
- M02.L02: Student can explain input brew ratio vs espresso beverage mass ratio and post-extraction bypass.
- M02.L03: Student develops multiple hypotheses for slow flow and selects one meaningful test.
- M02.L04: Student can define variables, controls, assumptions, safe trials and how to interpret a failed hypothesis.
- M02.L05: Student refuses to infer taste or EY from a ratio/time table that lacks measurement, then proposes next evidence.

Each check MUST be administered on a **new scenario**, with an independent attempt before feedback. A novice's incorrect answer is evidence to revise the explanation or media, not a reason to quietly simplify the assessment into trivia.
