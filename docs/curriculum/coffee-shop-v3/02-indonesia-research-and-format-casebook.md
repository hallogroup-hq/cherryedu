# Coffee Shop V3 — Indonesia Market Research & Format Cases

**Research checkpoint:** 16 October 2026 (Asia/Jakarta). Branch: `feat/coffee-shop-specialist-depth-v3`. This source ledger is internal, **not** student-facing.
**Purpose:** Differentiate verifiable facts, interpretation, and purely hypothetical calculations. Prohibit suggesting competitors' secret operating margins or claiming quoted sample costs are actual October 2026 offers.

## Five distinct operating formats

| Format | What learner must distinguish | Real illustrative company | Main decision drivers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sit-down coffee shop | Seats, customer dwelling, table service, cleaning, toilet, Wi-Fi, rent, utilities and occupancy | Independent hypothetical local owner | Dwell time, table turns, beverage+food mix, cleaning and service expectations |
| Compact take-away / coffee-to-go | Fast queue, minimal seating, pickup and packaging, commuter timing, channel economics | Tuku neighbourhood/short-form transaction as an **illustration**, not a claim all Tuku stores are identical | Grab-and-go conversion, handoff error, throughput and repeat |
| Multi-format chain | Systematic SOP, multi-store menu consistency, inventory, omnichannel and capital allocation | PT Fore Kopi Indonesia Tbk, directly documented format categories | Three sizes: flagship, medium, satellite; consistent KPI with different space cost |
| Neighbourhood repeat-led store | Familiar taste, neighbourhood relationship, recurring customers, local event touchpoints | Toko Kopi TUKU | Repeat cohort, community trust, accessible product, freshness and staffing |
| Electric cart / mobile coffee | Walking/driving route, depot restocking, operator travel time, safe electrical equipment, location legality, temperature control, GPS/app ordering | Jago Coffee | Sales per route hour, lost hours, re-supply, route density, payment, safe street access |

These are **operating format lenses**, not five mutually-exclusive company classifications. A chain may operate take-away and sit-down outlets; a local brand can use a cart; take-away can still have a few seats. No franchise or private ownership assumption.

## Source ledger, evidence classes and validity

1. **Public company filed document — Fore 2025 Annual Report**. Fore 2025 reports at year-end **316 coffee stores in Indonesia**, partitioned as **14 flagship, 204 medium, 98 satellite**. The reported group total is **322** including four Singapore coffee stores and two Indonesian donut stores. These are *December 2025 published historical facts*, not current-year or privately audited unit economics. The company also describes omni-channel through its own app and third-party Gojek/Grab, as well as physical stores. https://fore.coffee/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Annual_Report_FORE_2025.pdf ; https://fore.coffee/id/investors-annual-report-id/
2. **Brand first-party — TUKU**, about and store pages, observed October 2026. First shop at Cipete in 2015; reports the 'Bertetangga Baik' relationship approach, Kopi Susu Tetangga, local communities and consistency/fast service. **Not proof of retention rate, profit or operating margin**; no precise company financial information used. https://www.tuku.coffee/id/tentang-kami ; https://www.tuku.coffee/id/surat-kabar ; https://www.tuku.coffee/id/lokasi
3. **Developer first-party app listings — Jago Coffee / Jagoan**, accessed October 2026. Customer app describes an electric-cart coffee operation with mobile ordering and advertises some drinks **starting at Rp8,000**; price is *app's marketing statement*, not cost of production nor fixed universal October 2026 price. Jagoan operator app describes barista/courier handling orders, product stock and communications. Do **not** claim to know proprietary routes, funding, unit margins, exact number of active carts or public operating permits. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=id&id=com.jagocoffee.app ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=id&id=com.jagocoffee.jagoan
4. **Central bank policy — Bank Indonesia MDR QRIS**. BI 13 October 2026 publication says effective 1 October 2026: regular UMI transactions up to Rp500,000: **0%**; UMI >Rp500,000: **0.3%**; regular small/medium/large (UKE/UME/UBE) transactions up to Rp100,000: **0%**; >Rp100,000: **0.7%**. MDR borne by **merchant**, cannot be charged as a customer surcharge. Merchant categorization must be obtained from provider, not assumed from amount of sales. This is *QRIS MDR*, NOT delivery platform commission or generic wallet fees. https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/cerita-bi/Pages/mdr-qris.aspx ; https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2815926.aspx
5. **GoFood Merchant contractual help, updated August 7 2026**. Fee calculated from **net sale per transaction**, after merchant-funded discounts, excluding restaurant tax and certain separately agreed charges according to the merchant-specific form. An arbitrary 20% in a teaching example must be called hypothetical, not a market rate or universal contract. Verify merchant account statements. https://gofoodmerchant.co.id/bantuan/article/Seputar-pemotongan-komisi ; https://portal.gofoodmerchant.co.id/files/terms-and-condition/gofood-v08.2025
6. **Indonesia licensing/hygiene**, prior vetted course anchors: OSS KBLI 2025 and Permenkes 11/2025. Mobile carts require checking local trading/road/space rules, hygiene, power and water according to actual place/vehicle/use; it is incorrect to assert all city regulations are the same. https://oss.go.id/id ; https://jdih.kemkes.go.id/documents/peraturan-menteri-kesehatan-nomor-11-tahun-2025
7. **Wage benchmark issued by Indonesian authorities for 2026 (not real cafe salary survey).** Official West Java UMK decree 561.7/Kep.862-Kesra/2025 establishes Kota Sukabumi Rp3,192,807, Kabupaten Sukabumi Rp3,831,926 and Kota Bandung Rp4,737,678. DKI Jakarta provincial announcement puts UMP 2026 at Rp5,729,876. The Jabar decision explicitly addresses under-one-year employment, older employee wage scale and a micro/small business exception to be read with applicable labour law. These figures are **not** a turnkey salary quote, not a universal number across business types and are **not** a complete loaded employer cost (BPJS, leave, mandatory benefits, shift rules etc.). https://jdih.jabarprov.go.id/page/eksekusi_download/32/2025kg00320862.pdf ; https://www.jakarta.go.id/siaran-pers/6328-SP-HMS-12-2025
8. **Research still needed for real purchase prices / rent / vendor-specific wages / carts**: dated **three-way vendor quotes in target city**, lease offer with deposit and utilities, employment costs by city and employment type, supplier invoice, payment contracts and local fees. No direct verified supplier quotations obtained in this review. **All model-cost tables in lessons remain labelled latihan/hipotetis, not real Indonesian price surveys.**

## Model-case evidence policy

A public company fact is dated and cited. An **inference** is labelled 'pelajaran yang bisa diuji', not attributed as a proven causal company outcome. An **exercise with synthetic numbers** is labelled 'Angka latihan — bukan data keuangan [company]'. No invented brand revenues, customer counts, actual margins, platform take-rates or break-even figures. Names like Fore, TUKU and Jago are **examples of public business formats**, not endorsements or proof of commercial partnership with CherryEdu.
