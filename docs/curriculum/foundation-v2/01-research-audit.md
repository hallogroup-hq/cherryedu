## Executive assessment

- Inspected **32/32 Foundation lesson bodies** in 7 modules; roughly **17,455 words**. Word count is not quality.
- Editorial priority distribution: **8 P0, 23 P1, 1 P2**. P0 = stop re-publishing risky/obsolete claim; P1 = substantive revision; P2 = editorial relocation.
- Existing foundation module bank: **7 quizzes × 3 questions = 21 multiple-choice questions**. Legacy questions sometimes enforce scientifically overconfident premises, so must be replaced alongside lesson content.
- Major defect: science is taught as categorical one-to-one laws (altitude→taste, process→notes, Mg→floral), terminology overwhelms beginner goals, and factual recall is used as a stand-in for competence.
- Special note: no real sensory tests, blind trials, lab analyses, expert endorsement or user testing have been performed by this audit.

## Outcome gaps

1. Observation versus hypothesis: pupils must tell sensory observations from conjectures about origin/processing.
2. Multi-variable causal reasoning: identify confounding variables rather than trace every note to one farm factor.
3. Data literacy: interpret simple process/water records including uncertainty and measurement limits.
4. Risk thinking: identify hygiene, drying, processing and equipment red flags without receiving unreviewed industrial recipes.
5. Critical reading: distinguish traceability metadata, external verification, value statements, certification, and marketing claims.
6. Assessment: use case judgments, justification and spaced retrieval in addition to carefully written MCQ.

## Publication controls

- **P0 claims: zero unresolved prior to publication**.
- Every volatile standard, numeric prescription or high-stakes scientific claim requires a documented source version, context and expert review when applicable.
- A CoffeeEdu/CherryEdu course-completion certificate must not be represented as SCA/Q Grader certification.
- Passing an internal quiz is not proof of practical professional competency.
- Maintain a change log for standard revisions; preserve original text for comparison and rollback.
