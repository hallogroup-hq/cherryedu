## IV. Numerical QA / transfer validity

### M07.L03 teaching example — 100 kg sold in both cases

- Baseline: 100kg × Rp12.000 = Rp1.200.000; recorded costs Rp900.000 → **Rp300.000**.
- Premium: 100kg × Rp13.500 = Rp1.350.000; recorded costs Rp1.080.000 → **Rp270.000**.
- Revenue difference +Rp150.000; cost difference +Rp180.000; residual difference **−Rp30.000**.
- Interpret only as example-period residual after *recorded* costs, not verified farm net/living income.

### M07.L05 DISTINCT final transfer example — 100 kg harvested, *different volumes sold*

- Baseline: 100kg sold × Rp12.000 = Rp1.200.000; costs Rp900.000 → **Rp300.000** residual.
- Premium: only **90kg** sold × Rp15.000 = Rp1.350.000; costs Rp1.130.000 → **Rp220.000** residual.
- **10kg unaccepted/not sold** in premium scenario: fate, other receipts, time costs and handling fees UNKNOWN; no extra profit/loss invented for it.
- Revenue delta +Rp150.000; costs delta +Rp230.000; recorded residual delta **−Rp80.000**.
- Both numbers fictional. Replacing L03 exercise values with novel L05 values prevents passing solely by memorized prior answer.

### Final dossier brewing example

- Dry dose = 15g; brew water input = 240g; **output beverage mass = 210g**; beverage TDS=1.35%.
- Estimated solubles in cup = 210 × .0135 = **2.835g**.
- Estimated EY (collected beverage / dry dose) = 2.835 / 15 × 100 = **18.9%**.
- Using 240g input rather than 210g output is erroneous; sample uncertainty and retention must be acknowledged.
- Values **do not confer** sensory rating, food safety or commercial clearance.

## V. Claims and social impact ethics

| Misleading claim | Evidence required before making a narrower, accurate statement |
|---|---|
| “Direct trade means every cent goes to farmer” | Contract/payout and unit-specific chain-of-payments; clarify cooperative role and overhead |
| “High coffee price means higher farmer income” | Quantity actually sold, accepted/rejected volumes, complete cost coverage, household-income period |
| “Fairtrade label guarantees all farmers reached living income” | Certified scope/volume, price and payment data, producer welfare benchmark and measured household outcomes |
| “Single farm” with mixed owner records | Farm/sub-lot map, blend composition and chain-of-custody evidence |
| “Shade grown means carbon neutral/zero deforestation” | Land-use history, measured environmental indicators, system boundary, GHG accounting methodology |
| “EUDR-ready means fully legally compliant” | Responsible legal entity, product scope, dated regulatory requirements, verified due diligence evidence |
| “Roast log smooth/DTR ideal means CVA 90+” | Appropriate sensory methods and evaluator data, physical and extrinsic evidence where relevant |
| “11.2% moisture = no contamination risk” | Incident investigation, appropriate trained QC assessment and, if relevant, validated hazard testing |

**Important distinction:** “Unverified” is not synonymous with “false”; “plausible” is not synonymous with “proved”. Do not imply all conventional farmers violate labor law or that all sustainability schemes fail.

## VIII. Dated legal-scope correction — 12 October 2026

The original EUDR summary distinguished large/medium and micro/small actors but **omitted a separate 2027 timeline for newly listed products**. The official Commission Green Forum, July 2026 Commission announcement, and **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102** (published 17 September 2026) distinguish:

- Large/medium operators for pre-existing in-scope coffee product codes: **30 December 2026**.
- Most micro/small operators for pre-existing in-scope products: **30 June 2027**, subject to EUTR exception.
- Newly added CN **2101 11 00** coffee extracts, essences and concentrates (e.g., soluble coffee within the code): **30 December 2027**.

[M07.L04](../03-lessons/M07-L04-traceability-sustainability-klaim.md) now teaches **product-code × operator-size × legal-role × date**, not a universal coffee export deadline. Official references: [Commission overview](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/forests/deforestation/regulation-deforestation-free-products_en), [Green Forum role guidance](https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/nature-and-biodiversity/deforestation-regulation-implementation/roles-and-responsibilities_en), [EU 2026/2102](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R2102).
