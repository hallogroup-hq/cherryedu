**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-12  
**Scope:** 7 complete instructional drafts, 10-item module assessment, simulated lot memo; historical 32-lesson blueprint revised to **33** lessons because green coffee grading was a genuine missing competency.  
**Outcome:** **READY FOR EXPERT/LEARNER REVIEW**, NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION.

## I. Why Module 03 is seven lessons, not six

The previous six-slot sequence covered receiving and processing, washed/natural/honey, wet hulling, fermentation, drying/storage and a case. It **skipped the separate competency between drying and roasting: physical preparation, dry milling and inspection of green coffee**. That is central to the farm-to-cup promise.

New lesson **M03.L06** closes this gap, explicitly differentiating:
1. Dry mill preparation (hulling, cleaning, screening/sorting).
2. Physical assessment (defects, size, moisture, color).
3. Sensory/descriptive/affective assessment and extrinsic traceability.
4. Method and standards version versus buyer contract specification.
5. Sampling and uncertainty.

The former wet-mill scenario is now M03.L07, a combined **wet-mill → dry-mill lot-release reasoning task**. Therefore new Foundation topology = 3+5+7+4+5+4+5 = **33 unique lesson slots**. History remains M07.L01 **required**, not optional.

### Critical research gaps to close before technical release

- **G1** Get Indonesian wet-hulling academic/technical source or regional expert observation rather than industry blogs alone; correct any regional/history overreach.
- **G2** Commission processor/food safety SME review for CO₂, pressure, microbial/toxin and water-activity wording. This module must NEVER be presented as operator food safety training.
- **G3** Green grading professional to review defect examples, sampling and SCA CVA application. SCA-110 remains a monitored future change.
- **G4** Verify current Codex/Indonesian food safety/export requirements when introducing any binding product spec; avoid inventing compliance from generic texts.
- **G5** Additional grounded Indonesian dry mill and wet mill cases from real producers (with permission and anonymization).
- **G6** Learner pilot: minimum 5–8 beginner participants for qualitative early testing (not statistical proof); measure confusion and transfer tasks.
- **G7** Media: diagrams should illustrate cherry layer removal, the wet-hull handoff and drying/storage; photograph provenance/licensing and accurate labels need review.

## V. Beginner UX editorial review

Three possible friction points:
1. **Terminology density:** “activity air”, “toksin”, “metabolomics”, “sampling representatif”, “CVA physical” occur in the course. Need first-use definitions and optional glossary; don't drown first-time learners in abbreviations.
2. **Pacing:** Seven lessons plus 10-item exit is justified by the green QC gap, but time should be observed in pilot rather than assumed from word counts.
3. **Decision clarity:** HOLD and PROCEED FOR REVIEW are fictional classroom labels. Student might misread “review” as operational clearance; emphatically reinforce the distinction.

This is an editorial framework, NOT evidence of scientific peer review or certification.
