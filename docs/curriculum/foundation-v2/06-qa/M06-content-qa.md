**QA snapshot:** 2026-10-12  
**Scope:** Five fully authored beginner lessons, twelve module-exit MCQs, applied four-lens memo.  
**Status:** Internal desk review and source register. **Not** a professional sensory review, learner validation, or permission to publish.

## 1. Rationale for a five-lesson module

The original four-lesson design included sensory physiology, lexicon, comparative cupping and CVA framework, but **lacked an independently testable competency: separating sensory evidence from bias and establishing comparability/calibration**.

This omission is material because trainees might otherwise treat descriptor confidence or a fancy cupping term as evidence of objectivity. Recent controlled evidence (2026 processing label experiment) demonstrates that contextual information can modify responses even when the coffee is held constant.

**New M06.L03** explicitly covers label/price/cup-color expectation, repeatability vs accuracy, references, controlled comparisons and no deceptive learner experiments. Former L03 cupping becomes L04; former L04 CVA becomes L05.

Blueprint now **36 compulsory Foundation slots** (M01 3, M02 5, M03 7, M04 5, M05 6, M06 5, M07 5), including **mandatory coffee history M07.L01** and no deletion of legacy valuable topics.

## 4. Specific misconception/ethics checklist

- **M01 cross-ref:** Observation ≠ preference; visual/label expectations can modify judgments.
- **L01:** Taste basics ≠ aroma molecules; astringency ≠ acidity; descriptions ≠ composition, no invalid universal tongue map.
- **L02:** WCR lexicon ≠ complete memory test; word specificity and confidence are different; local foods as optional conceptual analogies only.
- **L03:** Bias ≠ lying; blind does not erase all bias; consistent reports ≠ validated sensory accuracy; **no nonconsensual deception of learners**.
- **L04:** Informal comparisons ≠ SCA-compliant CVA; hygiene (no used shared tasting spoon), safe temperature, no testing medical smell acuity.
- **L05:** Physical, descriptive, affective and extrinsic are distinct; SCA-105 now published; legacy 80+ retained historically; traceability marketing does not prove actual producer premium or carbon impact.
- **Asset licenses:** No copied Flavor Wheel, WCR full lexicon or official SCA forms in commercial content without verified rights.
- **Certificates:** Avoid official CVA/Q Grader/SCA-certified badges or promises.

## 6. Early-stage beginner UX and access

- **Possible friction:** Overlap between M01 and L01. Differentiate in UI: M01 is basic language; M06 adds sensory physiology, oral tactile concepts and greater specificity/structured recording.
- **Too much jargon risk:** orthonasal, retronasal, trigeminal, calibration, CATA, CVA. Use examples before glossary entries; optionally collapsible advanced definitions.
- **Local relevance:** use Indonesian fruit/herb descriptors as familiar comparisons **not validated substitutes** for standard panel references.
- **No-equipment:** synthetic cases work even for students who cannot taste caffeine, cannot access equipment, or choose not to engage with hot drinks.
- **Accessibility:** avoid using wheel colors as the only way to navigate descriptors; text categories and accessible answer feedback required.
- **Needed media:** side-by-side origin of aroma diagram and evidence four-lens diagram; original IP only, scientific fact-checked. Avoid decorative AI stock and copied SCA Wheel imagery.

## 8. Proposed corrective actions

1. **Sensory specialist reviewer** checks physiology, astringency, word hierarchy, sample comparison and the claims about biases.
2. **Current SCA CVA instructor/Q Grader** validates precisely what SCA-102 through SCA-105 cover and confirms professional credential boundaries.
3. **Content/IP counsel** reviews proposed UI assets before any official wheel, form, translation or purportedly “SCA-inspired” replica is displayed.
4. **Small novice formative pilot** (5–8 participants suggested as an initial qualitative sample only) tests whether learners separate descriptive vs affective and classify all four evidence types.
5. **Product/UX work** supplies accessible original visuals and inclusive no-equipment alternatives, not required for draft QA.
6. **Integration** needs compatibility review of old lesson IDs, quiz attempts, stored progress, certificates and versioning before touching code.
