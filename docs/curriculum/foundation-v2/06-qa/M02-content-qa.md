## 3. Audit of student experience

1. **Progression:** anatomy → naming/classification → genotype/environment management → maturity/Brix → integrated Indonesian farm case. This follows Stage 02 module slot sequence.
2. **Novice language:** technical names introduced after familiar concepts; question-centered, no forced jargon memorization.
3. **Context:** local names Sigarar Utang, Ateng, Tim-Tim presented cautiously as names requiring verified genetic provenance; no invented per-variety tasting notes.
4. **No-equipment accessibility:** exercises available in text; no refractometer, farm visit, coffee set or lab gear required.
5. **No-simulation deception:** fictitious farm and Brix data clearly marked.
6. **Safety:** no pesticide recipe, rust diagnosis, equipment pressure protocol or industrial food safety authority claimed.
7. **Assessment:** 8 MCQ stems with keys/rationales; one open memo. 7/8 (~87.5%) and 9/12 proposed as thresholds, not research-certified.
8. **Media pending:** M02.L01 requires accurately drawn anatomical cross-section to improve spatial understanding; this is a future **necessary** pedagogical asset, not decorative. A text-only alternative must remain complete. M02.L04 could benefit from legally sourced images of red and yellow mature cherries with botanical provenance.
