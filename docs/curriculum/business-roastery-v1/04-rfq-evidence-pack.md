## Supplier green-bean request
**Subject:** RFQ green bean lot [ORIGIN] — spesifikasi, sampel, MOQ dan terms

> Halo [pemasok],  
> Mohon penawaran tertulis green coffee bean siap roasting untuk [origin/lot], varietas/species, proses, crop/harvest date, kondisi pascapanen, grade/defect method, kadar air dan metode uji, profil cupping beserta tanggal sampel, traceability, kuantitas tersedia, minimum pembelian, harga dan masa berlaku, jenis packing, lokasi serah-terima, pajak relevan, DP/COD/termin, penggantian lot, serta hak pemeriksaan dan klaim bila sampel berbeda dari kiriman.  
>  
> Tolong pisahkan harga produk dari semua biaya pengiriman, cantumkan apakah barang sudah siap kirim serta kapan dapat diinspeksi dan diterima. Kami tidak menganggap quotation sebagai booking atau jaminan repeat lot.

## Standard evidence row for spreadsheets/finance lab
Capture in this order, one row per distinct quote:
1. **Provider and quote ID:** provider legal name, contact, quote number, date, expiry, validity restrictions.
2. **Commodity:** green coffee physical form, origin/lot, moisture and packing declaration; do not substitute cherry, gabah or roasted beans.
3. **Addresses and route:** full pickup/delivery with postal codes; actual direction; intermediate hubs and who bears each stage.
4. **Shipment:** actual kg, chargeable/volumetric kg and formula, minimum billed weight or flat truck/container, dimensions, service type and date.
5. **Terms:** included/excluded tax, packing, insurance, first/last-mile, loading, waiting/storage, surcharge, packing claims, lead time, payment and Incoterm-like domestic responsibility terms as applicable.
6. **Outcome:** total acquisition cost, received kg, QC rejects, supplier credits, roasted saleable kg, invoice/receipts and calculation date.
7. **Evidence status:** published indicative listing / carrier written quote / paid commercial invoice; never conflate these categories.

## Independent example: only base freight, not real delivered cost
For a *published* Makassar→Jabodetabek sea-cargo estimate **starting Rp3,500/kg, minimum 50 kg**, 35 kg triggers a **base indicative Rp175,000**. This does not establish shipment from Toraja, eligibility of green coffee, delivery to Sukabumi, pickup, tax, packing, insurance or insurance settlement. Additional charges remain **unknown until written quotation**.

IndoExpress's two accessible Jakarta→Medan references instead show **Rp7,900/kg, minimum 100 kg** (not 10 kg). A 5 kg parcel, **if this freight tariff is applicable**, faces a base indicator of Rp790,000 before exclusions. This illustrates why minimum volume matters; it is not a recommended shipping service for a 5 kg order.
