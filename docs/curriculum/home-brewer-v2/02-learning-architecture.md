# Home Brewer Specialization Path — V2 Architecture

## Learning promise
Bukan mencari “resep terbaik” dari senior atau juara kompetisi; peserta mampu menyeduh kopi enak **sesuai tujuan sendiri** di alat rumah yang berbeda, memahami mengapa resep berubah, mencatat apa yang diuji, membedakan hasil nyata dari prediksi, dan merancang signature home recipe/iced coffee tanpa mengorbankan keamanan.

**Audience:** new and intermediate home brewers (Indonesian), without expensive tools required. Home brewer is not assumed to operate professional espresso or steam equipment.

## Order (10 modules × 5 lessons = 50)
- HB01 — Memilih Kopi & Mendefinisikan Target Rasa (label, roast/process, freshness/storage, preferences, sourcing)
- HB02 — Membaca Angka Seduhan (scale, dose/water/output, ratios, strength vs extraction, experiment journal)
- HB03 — Variabel yang Benar-benar Berpengaruh (grind, temperature/hot-water safety, agitation, filter/pour, repeatability)
- HB04 — Pour-over yang Bisa Dijelaskan (brew setup, bloom, flow/bed, controlled changes, V60 troubleshooting)
- HB05 — Immersion, French Press & AeroPress (mechanisms, French Press, upright AeroPress, method comparison, method adaptation)
- HB06 — Diagnosa dan Penyesuaian Rasa (sensory vs measurement, confounders, fast/slow, data diagnosis, case lab)
- HB07 — Melatih Lidah & Preferensi (aroma/taste/body, tasting notes, blind comparisons, triangulation, coffee selection)
- HB08 — Air, Perawatan, dan Ekonomi Rumahan (safe potable water, minerals vs TDS, cleaning, stock/waste, accessible budgeting)
- HB09 — Iced Coffee, Milk & Home Beverage Ideas (iced dilution, safe cold brew, milk basics/allergens, original flavor idea, consistency)
- HB10 — Home Brewer R&D & Capstone (question/design, controlled iteration, troubleshoot unexpected data, recipe reproducibility, portfolio)

## Every lesson contract
1. Begin with a real home situation and a question a novice can ask.
2. Explain **mechanism before recipe** and explain jargon at first occurrence.
3. Illustrate with real calculations or explicitly hypothetical data; distinguish `dose`, `brew water`, `beverage output` and `bypass`.
4. Include a counterexample/alternative cause; never infer exact taste, chemistry, caffeine or safety from insufficient data.
5. Meaningful trial: prediction → one intentionally changed factor → observations → revision.
6. New transfer situation that cannot be solved by memorizing the example's answer.
7. Safe at-home method or dry/screen simulation; safety override.
8. Contextual visual placement and actual input/feedback interaction where justified; no pseudo-card/two-column template.

## Learning assessments
- 2 short reasoned diagnostics per module; feedback for every option, no answer by option length.
- 3 changing-evidence cases per module; an initial decision must precede the hidden new fact and revised response.
- Concept labs for measurable ratio, bypass and budget; outputs **not** literal taste predictors.
- Capstone: write a recipe for a chosen need, complete a transparent A/B proposal, record the result if physically tried, repeat or revise after surprise variable.
- Module-level feedback formative only; no automatic proof of practical skill or external professional certificate.

## Release constraints
- Video not required **for this version**, explicitly at owner's direction; never pretend video has been viewed.
- User is final academic validator; no tool can self-approve curriculum. Ownership of validation does not imply it was conducted.
- Visual diagrams and annotated sketches must be legible, accessible and accurate; still no substituting fabricated physical results.
- New path stays separate from legacy paid entitlement until access and progress migration has been designed and authorized.
- Technical QA: typecheck/build, content inventory, false certainty lint, browser coverage for every lesson, practice forms, mobile layout, and Foundation/legacy regressions.
- Only merge/release after owner validates content and product gate, even when CI is green.

## Evidence boundaries
SCA Brewing Control Chart is a framework mapping strength (TDS), extraction yield and mass ratio, not an obligation that every personal cup match a target box. AeroPress inverted is disallowed as a tutorial action because manufacturer discourages that method; accessible upright techniques only. Water minerals may influence extraction and perception but do not guarantee flavor or allow unreviewed DIY concentrates.
